## Upwork portfolio title

FDA Registration Data Cleaning & CRM Preparation — 16,373 Records

## Upwork description (paste)

Transformed a messy FDA medical-device registration extract (16,382 source rows) into 16,373 validated CRM-ready records: 10,636 Companies and 5,737 Contacts. Work included invalid-row detection with audit trail, entity separation, stable external IDs, Contact-to-Company linking, completeness scoring, duplicate review, QA checks, and field mapping for Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, and Dynamics 365.
